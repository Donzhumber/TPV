\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Identifying Rational Types in Unknown Environments under an Indirect Dynamic Mechanism} \\
Humberto Bernal
\end{center}

\vspace{2em}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Models strategic state-insurgent interactions (FARC, ELN, AUC) via dynamic indirect screening.
    \item Mano de Dios--Guadalupe process embeds maximum-entropy noise to sustain Bayesian updating under conflict friction.
    \item Rational Type Identification Theorem proves almost-sure posterior separation of ideological versus criminal insurgent types.
    \item Microeconometric competing-risks hazards calibrate empirical kidnapping dynamics across four decades of Colombian conflict.
    \item Counter-terrorism policy evaluation quantifies structural trade-offs between financial interdiction ($\alpha$) and tactical pressure ($\gamma$).
\end{itemize}
