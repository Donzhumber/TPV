\noindent I submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Identifying Rational Types in Unknown Environments under an Indirect Dynamic Mechanism''} for publication consideration in \textit{Terrorism and Political Violence}.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent In asymmetric conflicts, non-state armed groups conceal their capabilities, organizational structure, and ideological motivation. When direct communication fails, strategic interactions between the state and armed actors produce observational pooling. To analyze this problem, this paper formulates an indirect dynamic screening mechanism. The model introduces the Mano de Dios Guadalupe (MDG) implementation kernel, a maximum entropy stochastic formulation that maintains full support across executed actions and preserves well defined off-path Bayesian updating in Perfect Bayesian Equilibrium.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The primary analytical contribution is the Rational Type Identification Theorem. The theorem proves that under recurrently separated signals, the trajectory measures of distinct armed factions are mutually singular. As a result, the state planner's posterior beliefs converge almost surely to the true latent type of the group during prolonged captivity, separating ideological guerrillas from criminal syndicates without direct revelation reports.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The framework is calibrated using microdata from Colombia's Centro Nacional de Memoria Historica on kidnappings conducted by the FARC, ELN, paramilitaries, and criminal groups. Using competing risks duration models and deep learning value function approximations, the paper evaluates policy trade-offs between financial interdiction ($\alpha$) and tactical operational pressure ($\gamma$). All mathematical proofs are verified in the Lean 4 interactive theorem prover, with complete replication materials hosted at \url{https://github.com/Donzhumber/TPV.git} and an interactive simulation tool available at \url{https://7kkgn4sfscykkfvv5794qw.streamlit.app/}.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The manuscript is original and is not under review elsewhere. Anonymized and named files have been prepared in compliance with double-blind peer review requirements. Thank you for your consideration.
